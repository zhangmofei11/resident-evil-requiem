Boss Guides

Defeat every boss with proven strategies and attack pattern breakdowns.

🧟

Plague Biter

Chapter 4 - Mutated hospital patient

Easy

Attack Patterns

  • • Charge: Lunges forward, easily dodged
  • • Swipe: Wide melee attack, back up
  • • Spit: Acid projectile, strafe sideways

Strategy

  • • Shoot legs to stagger
  • • Focus head when staggered
  • • Use shotgun for fast kills
💪

G-Birkin

Chapter 7 - Mutated Umbrella experiment

Medium

Attack Patterns

  • • Grab: Massive arm reach, dodge backward
  • • Slam: Ground pound, run to sides
  • • Tentacle: Ranged attack, take cover

Strategy

  • • Shoot the core on chest when exposed
  • • Avoid the green mist
  • • Magnum is highly effective
👤

Commander Marcus

Chapter 8 - Human antagonist with combat training

Medium

Attack Patterns

  • • Machine Gun: Sustained fire, take cover
  • • Grenade: Explosive projectiles, dodge
  • • Melee: Quick close-range attacks

Strategy

  • • Use cover effectively
  • • Shoot during his reload windows
  • • Flash grenades stun him
🔬

Mother潜

Chapter 9 - The true final boss, mutated bio-weapon

Hard

Attack Patterns

  • • Tentacle Slam: Area attack, dodge roll
  • • Bio Explosion: Poison clouds, run away
  • • Summon: Spawns lesser enemies
  • • Core Beam: Charged laser, hide behind pillars

Strategy

  • • Destroy tentacles first
  • • Shoot the core when exposed
  • • Use Grenade Launcher for burst damage
  • • Save Magnum for final phase
👹

The Director

Chapter 10 - Human form, final phase

Hard

Attack Patterns

  • • Blade Combo: Fast sword attacks, block or dodge
  • • Guns: Fires handgun rapidly
  • • Desperation: Enraged mode, faster attacks

Strategy

  • • Stay close to avoid guns
  • • Dodge his sword combos
  • • Counterattack after his combos
  • • Headshots do extra damage

Boss Fight Tips

Save Ammo

Bosses take a lot of hits. Keep spare ammo and use weak weapons first.

Learn Patterns

Watch the wind-up animations. Every attack has a safe window.

Use Environment

Explosive barrels, cover spots, and escape routes are your friends.
