Characters Guide

All characters, enemies, and villains in Resident Evil Requiem.

Playable Characters

👤

Grace Ashcroft

FBI Technical Analyst and main protagonist. Daughter of Alyssa Ashcroft from Resident Evil Outbreak.

First-Person Stealth Technical

Chapters 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 playable

🎖️

Leon S. Kennedy

Legendary Rookie Agent. Returns for his first mainline appearance since RE6.

Third-Person Action Veteran

Chapters 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10 playable
