Story files reveal lore about the Ashcroft family, Umbrella's experiments, and the true nature of the outbreak. Reading all files unlocks the "Historian" achievement.

Treasures & Gems

Red Gem Locations

  • • Hotel Lobby: Behind reception desk
  • • Sewers: In drainage pipe
  • • RPD Station: On desk in Records Room
  • • Hospital: Inside locker

Gold Treasures

  • • Statue Eyes: Remove from pedestals
  • • Antique Coin: Various merchant locations
  • • Ancient Vase: Break to find gem inside
  • • Gem Necklace: Hidden in secret rooms

SOS Signals

Find all 15 SOS signals to unlock the "Lone Survivor" costume for Grace. These are distress beacons left by other survivors.

SOS #1-5: Chapters 1-3
SOS #6-10: Chapters 4-7
SOS #11-15: Chapters 8-10
