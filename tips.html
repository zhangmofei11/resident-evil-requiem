Tips & Tricks

Essential advice for new players to survive Raccoon City.

Combat Tips

🎯 Aim for the Head

Headshots deal 2x damage and can instant-kill most zombies. Practice precision in early chapters.

🔫 Manage Ammo

Ammo is scarce. Use knives for single zombies, save guns for groups or bosses.

🏃 Know When to Run

Not every fight is necessary. Running past enemies saves ammo and time.

💣 Use Grenades Wisely

Save explosives for emergencies or boss fights. Flash grenades are invaluable against tough enemies.

Survival Tips

❤️ Healing Priority

Green Herb + Red Herb combo is best. Only heal when below 50% health to maximize item efficiency.

🎒 Inventory Management

Split items between characters if playing co-op. Each can carry 12 slots. Prioritize heals and ammo.

📍 Typewriter Saves

Save often at typewriters. They also fully restore health. Bring typewriter ribbons from merchant.

🛒 The Merchant

Sell gems and treasures for cash. Prioritize buying weapon upgrades and healing items.

Stealth Tips (Grace)

  • • Crouch to move silently - Hold the crouch button to reduce noise
  • • Avoid lights - Enemies spot you faster in lit areas
  • • Use environmental distractions - Throw items to lure enemies
  • • Headshots from stealth - One-shot kills on most enemies
  • • Flash Grenades are clutch - Buy several for emergency escapes

Leon-Specific Tips

  • • Parry when possible - Leon's counter-attack deals massive damage
  • • Use movement cancels - Cancel reload animations with melee
  • • Partner up with Grace - Some doors require both characters
  • • Shotguns are life - Upclose power is Leon's specialty

Difficulty Selection

Assisted

Enemy awareness reduced, auto-aim available, infinite continues.

Standard

Balanced experience, recommended for first playthrough.

Hardcore

No HUD, limited saves, enemies are smarter. For veterans only.
