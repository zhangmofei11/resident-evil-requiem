VIRUS GUIDE

Bio-weapons and infection mechanics

🦠 T-VIRUS

Basic Properties

  • • Progenitor virus strain
  • • Causes rapid cell mutation
  • • Fatal to most humans
  • • Creates reanimation after death

Infection Effects

  • • Aggressive behavior changes
  • • Loss of higher brain function
  • • Extreme physical strength
  • • No pain response

👁️ G-VIRUS

Characteristics

  • • Mutation-inducing pathogen
  • • Creates unique creatures
  • • Host-specific mutations
  • • Highly unstable

Mutation Stages

  • • Initial transformation
  • • Limb regeneration
  • • Complete body restructure
  • • Final form emergence
